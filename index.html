Zizo World 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game Zizo World 3D (package com.zizoworld.adventure), published by Gamble Entertainment.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, selected character and settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. You can also erase it from Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission for short feedback when the character is hit. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect any data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
